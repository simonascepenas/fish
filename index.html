Add fish

The tank holds 10 fish. A well-fed juvenile is fully grown in 10 to 15 minutes.

Cleaners

Up to three. They are never fed: they live on algae, leftovers and a little of your plants, so the water stays clean for longer. Three snails keep the glass clean by themselves as long as you change the water often; in murky water they slow down and the glass goes green.

What each fish eats

The large orange dot is its favourite, which is what the auto-feeder serves.

The nerite snail and the bristlenose pleco eat none of these. They live on the algae on the glass, leftovers on the sand and a little of the plants.

Add a plant

Pick one, then tap the sand. Plants start as sprouts and grow in the light.

Tap the water to drop the selected food. Touch a fish and it darts away. Upkeep: change the water when it turns murky, drag across the glass to wipe off algae, and trim plants when they get overgrown: tap a plant for its panel, or use Trim at the top. A snail or a pleco (under Add fish) eats algae, leftovers and a little of the plants, so all of that is needed less often. Tap a fish to see its belly, health and size. A hungry fish shows the foods it eats in a bubble, and Diets lists them for every species. A fully grown fish is netted, fried and eaten, which frees its place in the tank. Keys 1 to 4 pick a food, space feeds.
